The proposed xxhash contract bounds `total≤32`, checks seed-derived lanes for `total<32` and unused tail, and preserves the old receiver on rejection. Boundary32 needs an explicit structural rule. Two algorithms may satisfy the contract, but two positive labels are not preassigned. If using a `purego` build, freeze selected sources/settings and make no SIMD benchmark claim.

The Chi contract specifies `GET /u/{id}`, empty RoutePath override, opt-in and both URL fields. With no RawPath, already interpreted Path must not be decoded again; `+` stays literal. URLParam and PathValue must agree after matching, while original URL and opt-out behavior remain intact. Malformed escapes, regex, mounts and context overrides are outside the first contract. A separate post-registration-panic case remains on hold because public APIs may not expose internal tree mutations; source control flow alone cannot assign a negative label.
